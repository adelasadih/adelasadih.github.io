PROJECT 04 · COMPETITION

Mahdis Library and Book Exhibition

Location: Ahvaz, Iran
Area: 1,700 m²
Floors: 3
Year: 2011

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Mahdis Library and Book Exhibition was developed as part of an architectural competition for a library and book exhibition facility in Ahvaz, Iran, a city characterized by a hot and humid climate.

The aim was to create a cultural space that would be functional, climatically responsive and inviting. The design process began with a simple rectangular building volume that was divided into three horizontal layers.

The lowest level was designed as a semi-open exhibition space, allowing natural ventilation and a direct connection to the surrounding outdoor environment. The entrance area was emphasized through a slightly lowered floor level.

The two upper levels, accommodating the library functions and a small administrative area, were rotated 90 degrees relative to each other. This rotation created overhanging elements that provided shade while enriching the architectural expression of the building.

Carefully positioned openings were introduced on the upper levels to promote daylight and natural ventilation while also creating vertical spatial connections between the different layers of the building.

At ground level, the structural columns were transformed into colourful vertical panels that served both structural and architectural purposes. These elements, using primary colours such as yellow, blue and green, helped organize the exhibition areas and strengthened the visual identity of the building.

Role: Co-Designer · Concept Development · Volumetric Design · Final Presentation

Team: Two-person design team.

Responsibilities: I actively participated throughout all stages of the competition project, from the initial design development and volumetric modelling to the final presentation.
