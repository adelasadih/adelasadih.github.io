PROJECT 02

Sohrabi Commercial Complex

Location: Tabriz, Iran
Area: 7,080 m²
Floors: 5
Year: 2022

PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Sohrabi Commercial Complex is located on Nezam-Pezeshki Street in the western part of Tabriz, Iran.

The project comprises five levels. The second basement level is dedicated to parking, while the first basement, ground floor, first floor and second floor are planned for commercial uses.

Due to the generous floor-to-floor heights, each level also includes an additional mezzanine covering approximately 50% of the floor area. This arrangement was developed to respond to the different functional requirements of the commercial units.

One of the client's main requirements was to create generous commercial units with high ceilings. This created logistical challenges, particularly for the transportation of goods. To address this issue, a large vehicle lift was designed on the western side of the project, accessed from a secondary street, providing convenient access to the upper commercial levels.

The main entrance and foyer are located on the eastern side at street level and are equipped with three elevators for visitors and customers. Since the parking levels are located two floors below ground level, a separate access ramp was planned from the eastern side.

One of the main challenges during the design process was the selection of appropriate façade materials. The client initially preferred contemporary materials such as composite panels and glass, or combinations of glass and natural stone. During the first design phase, several alternatives using composite materials, glass, stone and metal panels were developed.

Considering the project's location within one of the historic districts of Tabriz and its proximity to traditional brick-production areas, the design approach shifted towards the use of local and sustainable materials such as brick and natural stone.

This context-sensitive and environmentally conscious approach was presented to the client and was ultimately accepted.
