PROJECT 03

Atay Residential Complex

Location: Tabriz, Iran
Area: 8,645 m²
Floors: 13
Year: 2018

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Atay Residential Complex is a 13-storey residential development located in the Nasr district in eastern Tabriz, Iran.

The first basement level is used for parking, while the ground floor contains the lobby and additional parking spaces. The first three upper floors contain two residential units per floor, while the upper floors were designed as spacious single-unit apartments, with one apartment occupying each floor.

One of the main challenges of the project was the unusual triangular shape of the site, combined with differences in elevation along its different sides. Despite these constraints, the design aimed to provide fourteen residential units across eleven residential floors according to the client's requirements.

The upper residential units, with areas of up to 666 m², benefit from views towards landscaped areas. Generous terraces with different sizes and orientations were therefore incorporated along various sides of the façades.

In the floor plan organization, particular attention was given to the clear separation of private areas, including bedrooms, bathrooms and WCs, from semi-public zones such as the living room and kitchen. The reception area, with direct access to the main terrace, was defined as a public space.

Another challenge was the influence of the building form on the structural system, particularly due to the central placement of the staircase and elevator core within the triangular floor plan.

The façade was designed entirely in a classical architectural style. The distinction between the floors was mainly expressed through the choice of materials: the lower three floors were clad in natural stone, while the upper floors were finished with coloured cement and a waterproof epoxy coating.

Developing a façade design with a classical rhythm, proportion and symmetry despite the different floor plan configurations was one of the key design challenges of the project.

Role: Design Team Member · Execution Planning · 3D Modelling · Architectural Rendering · Construction Coordination

Professional Context: Developed during my employment at Ista-Frame Tarahan-e-Tabriz, Tabriz, Iran.
